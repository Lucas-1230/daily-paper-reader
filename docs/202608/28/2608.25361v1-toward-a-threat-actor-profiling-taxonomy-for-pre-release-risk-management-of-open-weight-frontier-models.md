## 摘要
目前针对前沿AI滥用风险的发布前风险管理通常使威胁行为者假设处于隐含、不一致或缺乏依据的状态。本文认为，明确的对手特征描述应被视为评估可解释、可比较且忠实于其所针对风险的前提。我们提出一个六属性分类法（涵盖技术熟练程度、先前领域知识、组织能力、操作基础设施、财务能力和时间范围），并依据现有恐怖主义、生物安全和网络安全文献构建了基于实证的分层等级。该分类法旨在充当研究基础设施：一种在评估开展之前预先指定对手假设的共同语言，类似于医学和经济学中随机对照试验的预分析计划。对于开放权重模型开发者而言，其应用尤为紧迫，因为他们的发布决策不可逆转，且必须预判对抗性推理。

## Abstract
Pre-release risk management for frontier AI misuse risks routinely leaves threat actor assumptions implicit, inconsistently specified, or ungrounded. This capstone argues that explicit adversary characterization should be regarded as a prerequisite for evaluations that are interpretable, comparable, and faithful to the risks they target. We propose a six-attribute taxonomy (covering technical sophistication, prior domain knowledge, organizational capacity, operational infrastructure, financial capacity, and time horizon) with empirically grounded tiers derived from existing terrorism, biosecurity, and cybersecurity literature. The taxonomy is designed to function as research infrastructure: a common language for pre-specifying adversary assumptions before evaluations are conducted, analogous to pre-analysis plans for randomized controlled trials (RCTs) in medicine and economics. Its application is particularly urgent for open-weight model developers, for whom release decisions are irreversible and must anticipate adversarial reasoning.
