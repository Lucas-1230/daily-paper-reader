## 摘要
AI辅助的网络安全系统以攻击者和防御者之间的持续适应为特征，使演化博弈论成为研究其长期行为的自然框架。然而，现有的演化网络安全模型主要关注同质交互，对种群结构如何影响网络攻击-防御动态的理解有限。在本文中，我们开发了一个混合角色演化博弈，其中自适应网络代理可以表现出攻击和防御两种行为，并研究其在均匀混合和结构化种群中的动态。所提出的框架结合了随机演化分析与大规模基于代理的模拟，以考察交互结构如何塑造长期策略行为。我们的结果揭示了全局和局部交互之间的根本差异。均匀混合种群展示了攻击和防御策略之间的广泛共存，而结构化种群生成了明确定义的演化机制，在该机制下，安全防御行为在参数空间的更大区域上占主导地位。空间分析进一步表明，相邻的防御者自然地形成弹性集群，通过网络互惠抑制持续攻击。这些发现表明，交互结构是AI辅助网络安全演化的基本决定因素，并建议通过局部网络化交互组织防御代理可以显著提高长期网络弹性，而无需额外的防御激励。

## Abstract
AI-assisted cybersecurity systems are characterised by continuous adaptation between attackers and defenders, making evolutionary game theory a natural framework for studying their long-term behaviour. However, existing evolutionary cybersecurity models have primarily focused on homogeneous interactions, providing limited understanding of how population structure influences cyber attack-defence dynamics. In this paper, we develop a mixed-role evolutionary game in which adaptive cyber agents can exhibit both offensive and defensive behaviours, and investigate its dynamics in well-mixed and structured populations. The proposed framework combines stochastic evolutionary analysis with large-scale agent-based simulations to examine how interaction structure shapes long-run strategic behaviour. Our results reveal a fundamental difference between global and local interactions. While well-mixed populations exhibit broad coexistence between attacking and defensive strategies, structured populations generate well-defined evolutionary regimes in which secure defensive behaviour becomes dominant over a much larger region of the parameter space. Spatial analysis further shows that neighbouring defenders naturally form resilient clusters that suppress persistent attacks through network reciprocity. These findings demonstrate that interaction structure is a fundamental determinant of AI-assisted cybersecurity evolution and suggest that organising defensive agents through local networked interactions can substantially improve long-term cyber resilience without requiring additional defensive incentives.
